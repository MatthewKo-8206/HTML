01 — The Story

From a small town
to everywhere

I grew up across Auckland, New Zealand, Douliu — a regional town in Taiwan where my family has roots — and Dubbo, NSW, where I was one of the only Asian kids in a town of 60,000.

Growing up between three places taught me to read rooms, adapt, and find direction without a script. I spent most of my time on piano, chess, and books. At 14, I won a place at Sydney Boys High School. At 17, I started a finance and economics degree at the University of Sydney.

"I was lucky enough to receive the DFAT New Colombo Plan Scholarship — $143,000 to study and work across Asia. I wanted to challenge myself as deeply as I could: to discover who I was, what I actually wanted to build, and define what success looked like on my own terms — outside the structures that had always defined me."

A year across Singapore and Taiwan changed everything. I self-taught coding from zero — 143 commits and a working multi-agent pipeline in 30 days. I started two companies. This summer: Evercore Restructuring EMEA, London. After that, EF London. Then: something of my own.

05 — The World

A year across Asia,
self-directed

The DFAT New Colombo Plan was my first time living abroad alone. I was 20. I arrived in Singapore not knowing what I was doing. A year and a half later, I left with close friends from Europe, the Americas, Asia, Africa and Oceania — people who are still in my life — and a version of myself I hadn't met before.

Jun–Aug 2025 · Singapore

ANZ Singapore · FIG

Six weeks on the FIG desk. I built the flagship ASEAN positioning deck that went to the CEO. Met Nuno Matos, the incoming Group CEO, and attended events at the Australian High Commission. Weekends meant midnight hawker stalls and exploring as much of the city as I could.

Aug–Dec 2025 · Singapore

National University of Singapore

The semester that changed everything. I made close friends from all over Europe, the Americas and Asia simultaneously — people with entirely different worldviews and frames for the same problems. I started to understand that the most interesting people were building things, not just managing them.

Feb–Jun 2026 · Taipei

I walked in not knowing how to code. Within 30 days: 143 commits, a working brand report pipeline, and SentimentDB — a Python wrapper I built across 466k products. The founder didn't ask me to do most of it. I kept going because I couldn't stop.
